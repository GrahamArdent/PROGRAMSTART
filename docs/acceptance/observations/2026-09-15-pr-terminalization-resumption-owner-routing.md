## What happened

A Home Automation learning observation was published as PROGRAMSTART PR #114. Its exact-head Required PR Gate completed successfully while the originating conversation moved on. The PR remained open until the operator later asked why it had not merged. Once the flow was explicitly resumed, currentness, focused Challenge, expected-head merge, and post-merge verification completed cleanly.

A retrospective PROGRAMSTART check found PR #93 had the same stronger pattern: its exact-head Required PR Gate succeeded on 2026-09-04, but the observation-only PR remained open and later mainline evolution turned what had been cheap closure into a diverged/non-mergeable candidate requiring reconciliation.

Counterevidence matters: open PROGRAMSTART PRs #112, #111, #104 and #101 had failed Required PR Gates when checked. Their remaining open state is not a terminalization defect and is evidence against universal auto-merge.

The cross-project finding was preserved in `GrahamArdent/portfolio-operations/IDEA_LEDGER.md` as `IDEA-pr-terminalization-resumption-ownership` before methodology evaluation.

**Adversarial sequence 1 — unsafe auto-merge:** A PR reaches green CI but still requires currentness, Challenge, stronger approval, or has contradictory evidence. Automatically merging on `green` would violate current authority. Therefore the required behavior is terminal **disposition/resumption**, not terminal auto-merge.

**Adversarial sequence 2 — green candidate loses owner:** The originating process/session ends while CI is pending. CI later becomes green. No durable continuation owner resumes the exact candidate, so it ages into stale-base debt. This is the observed #114/#93 failure mode.

**Adversarial sequence 3 — portfolio becomes executor:** Portfolio attention detects the stranded PR and directly merges it. That would make a derived attention surface execution authority. Correct behavior is to route/resume through the actual owning project/Controller semantics.
