## Freshness policy assessment

The seven-day limit remains in force for all ten tracks. It is a maintenance deadline, not evidence that every recommendation becomes false on day eight. Its blanket use as a strict repository gate couples unrelated changes to research maintenance; that is a real design tradeoff, not a reason to silently bypass the gate.

For this slice, complete an actual bounded review, record changed/unchanged decisions and follow-ups, then mark the reviewed tracks. Do not extend freshness windows, fabricate review dates or disable strict validation. A future policy change should be adopted in `docs/knowledge-ops.md` and the ledger together, based on observed maintenance effort and risk-specific update triggers. It should distinguish an overdue review from a verified broken or insecure recommendation and retain urgent-change handling. This report does not enact that change.

Review completion is recorded through the existing `programstart research --track <name> --mark-reviewed --date 2026-10-27` command. Unresolved project-specific adoption work above remains unresolved after the review date changes.
